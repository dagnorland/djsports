djSports Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

djSports is an app for DJs at sports events. It plays music through your own Spotify or Apple Music account. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes. It applies to djSports on Android, iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Summary

  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no selling of data.
  • Your playlists, tracks and start times are stored on your device.
  • The app talks directly to Spotify and Apple Music for login, search and playback.
  • Data only leaves your device for those services, and when you choose to make a cloud backup.

Data stored on your device

  • Playlists, tracks, start times, shortcuts and app settings.
  • Spotify login tokens, and the Spotify Client ID and Client Secret if you enter your own in Settings.

This data stays on the device. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Spotify

When you connect Spotify, you log in on Spotify's own login page. The app then uses the Spotify API to read your profile (display name, user ID, email address and subscription type), list your playback devices, search the catalog, read your playlists and control playback. This data goes directly between your device and Spotify. It is shown in the app but not sent to us. Spotify's handling of your data is covered by the Spotify Privacy Policy.

Apple Music (iPhone, iPad and Mac)

Apple Music access uses Apple's MusicKit with your permission. Search and playback go directly between your device and Apple. See the Apple Privacy Policy.

Cloud backup (optional)

If you tap "Back up" on the Cloud Backup screen, the app uploads a copy of your djSports data to Google Firebase Cloud Firestore so you can restore it later or on another device. A backup contains:

  • Your playlists, tracks and start times
  • The device name you chose
  • Your Spotify user ID and display name
  • The time of the backup, the app version and item counts

Backups are stored under a code calculated from your profile name and PIN. The profile name and PIN themselves are not stored, so only someone who knows both can find, restore or delete the backups.

The app keeps at most five backups per device and deletes the oldest one automatically. You can delete any backup yourself on the Cloud Backup screen. Backups are never made without your action. Firebase is operated by Google; see Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Permissions

  • Internet / network state: to reach Spotify, Apple Music and the cloud backup.
  • Foreground service (media playback) on Android: to keep playback controls working while the app is in the background.

Children

djSports is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
